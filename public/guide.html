How to play a match with Vault

Vault holds both players' buy-ins in a public smart contract and pays the winner automatically. Nobody, including this site, holds the money or takes a cut. Setup takes about ten minutes the first time, and about a minute after that.

1. One-time setup (both players)

  1. Use a computer. Vault needs a wallet in your browser. Chrome, Brave, Firefox and Edge all work.
  2. Install MetaMask from metamask.io/download, the official site, and create a new wallet.
    Your recovery phrase is the key to your wallet. Write it on paper and keep it offline. Never type it into any website, and never send it to anyone, including us. Nobody legitimate will ever ask for it.
  3. Get free test ETH. Matches run on Base Sepolia, a practice network where the coins have no value. In MetaMask, click your account name to copy your address (it starts with 0x). Then use one of the free faucets listed on Base's official faucet page. Pick "Base Sepolia" and paste your address.

    You need the buy-in, plus 10% of it in case you ever need the safety countdown, plus a tiny amount for network fees.

  4. No need to set up the network by hand. When Vault opens, it offers a Switch to the practice network button that does it for you.

2. Start a match (the host)

  1. Go to poker.slipgate.me and tap Heads-up — play with real stakes near the bottom of the page.
  2. Under Stakes, choose Crypto stakes and type the buy-in in ETH, for example 0.001. Crypto matches are always freezeouts: you play until one player has all the chips.
  3. Tap Create a table and send the invite link (or let your opponent scan the QR code).
  4. Wait until you see "Opponent's wallet received."
  5. Tap the Buyin button at the table. A Vault tab opens. Tap Connect your wallet to start, then switch to the practice network if asked.
  6. Under Step 1: Set up the match, tap Send opponent’s info and deposit my share and approve it in MetaMask.

Changed your mind?

Until your opponent deposits, you can tap Changed your mind? Cancel and get your money back.

3. Join a match (the opponent)

  1. Open the invite link from the host.
  2. Under Your wallet address, tap Use my wallet and approve in MetaMask. You can also paste your address instead. This tells the host which wallet to set the match up for.
  3. Tap Buyin at the table, then Connect your wallet to start.
  4. Once the host has deposited, you'll see Step 2: Put your money in. Tap Deposit my share and approve in MetaMask.
  5. Tap Go to the poker table → and play.

4. When the match ends: get paid

  1. The moment one player has all the chips, both poker tables sign the result automatically. You don't need to click anything.
  2. The Vault tab comes to the front. Either player taps Pay the winner and approves in MetaMask.
  3. You'll see "All done — the money has already been sent to the winner." The winner receives both buy-ins in full. There's no rake.

Want another match? Start a new game at the poker table and tap Buyin again. Every match gets its own fresh deposit.

If your opponent vanishes

Closing the tab can't get anyone out of a losing match. If your opponent stops responding:

  1. In the Vault tab, tap Start the countdown (opponent isn’t responding). This puts down a deposit of 10% of the buy-in and starts a 30-minute clock.
  2. If they don't answer in time, tap Finish the claim (only works after the countdown ends). You get the whole pot plus your 10% back.
  3. If they tap I'm actually here — stop the countdown, your claim was wrong and they keep your 10%. The match then has to be confirmed by both of you within a second countdown, or the original claim wins anyway.

So only use the countdown when your opponent is really gone, not just thinking about a hand.

Troubleshooting

The Vault tab didn't open

Your browser probably blocked the pop-up. Allow pop-ups for poker.slipgate.me, then tap Buyin again.

I closed the Vault tab by accident

Nothing is lost: the money and the result live on the blockchain, not in the tab. Tap Buyin at the table to reopen it.

MetaMask never popped up / "Nothing happened?"

Click the MetaMask fox icon in your browser's toolbar. The request is often waiting there. If it's truly stuck, tap Nothing happened? Give up on this and try again.

"Your opponent set this up for a different wallet"

You're connected with a different MetaMask account than the one you sent to the host. Switch accounts in MetaMask and reload the Vault tab.

Not enough ETH

Get more from a faucet (see setup step 3). Remember to keep a little extra for network fees.
